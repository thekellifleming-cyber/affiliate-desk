Post once on TikTok. Affiliate Desk handles Facebook.

A tool for affiliate creators (TikTok Shop, Amazon, Walmart and Sam's Club) that saves time on cross-posting and link work.

What it does

  • Lists your recent TikTok videos so you can repurpose them to your Facebook Page.
  • Adds your own affiliate links as comments on your Facebook reels, with the right disclosure.
  • Reminds you which TikTok videos still need a TikTok Shop product link.
  • Helps you answer comments on your Facebook Page.

Nothing is posted without your action or the settings you choose. Affiliate Desk is in private beta.
