Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

This policy explains what Affiliate Desk ("the app"), run by Kelli Fleming, collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • From TikTok, when you connect it: your basic profile (display name and an account ID) and the list of your public videos (titles, dates, cover images, links and view, like, comment and share counts).
  • From Facebook, when you connect it: the Pages you choose, your Page's reels and their comments, and permission to post reels and comments to those Pages.
  • What you enter: your name, email, product names and your affiliate links.
  • Login tokens: the access tokens TikTok and Facebook give the app, stored securely so it can act for you. We never see or store your TikTok or Facebook password.

How we use it

Only to run the features you use: showing your videos, posting to your Facebook Page, adding your links and disclosures, reminding you about TikTok Shop links and helping you reply to comments. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Sharing

Your data is sent only to the services needed to do what you ask (TikTok, Facebook, and our hosting and database providers). We share it otherwise only if the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is active. You can disconnect TikTok or Facebook at any time in the app or in their settings. To delete your account and all your data, email thekellifleming@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and we'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Security

Tokens and data are stored with access controls and sent over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect to what the app needs.

Children

The app is not for anyone under 18.

Changes

We'll update the date above when this policy changes.
